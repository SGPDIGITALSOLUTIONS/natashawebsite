Hudson Virtual — SEO implementation summary
14 October 2026

Repository: SGPDIGITALSOLUTIONS/natashawebsite
Public website: https://www.hudsonvirtual.co.uk/

The changes improve how search engines discover, understand and present Hudson
Virtual's public services. They also make service information and FAQ answers
available without requiring JavaScript or mouse hover.

Implemented

- Unique titles, descriptions, self-referencing canonical URLs and social
  metadata for the seven existing pages and six new service pages.
- A sitemap listing the 13 canonical public pages, plus robots.txt referencing
  it. No artificial content-modification dates are emitted on every build.
- A permanent redirect from the apex host to www while preserving the path.
- Noindex controls for Vercel preview deployments; production remains indexable.
- Six dedicated service pages based on the services already offered on the site:
  executive administration, board support, personal assistance, operations
  management, event management and bespoke support.
- Direct links from the homepage and services overview to those service pages,
  plus links to pricing, FAQs, related services and the consultation form.
- Organization, Person and WebSite structured data with consistent identifiers.
  Service and BreadcrumbList data appear on the relevant service pages. No
  ratings, outcomes or new testimonials have been invented.
- Native FAQ disclosure controls that support keyboard and touch interaction
  and keep every answer in the initial HTML. The on-demand minimum now agrees
  with the existing pricing page: three hours.
- Static server rendering for the informational About and Testimonials pages,
  removing their animation library from the client page bundles.
- Locally hosted Montserrat with its OFL licence and font-display swap, removing
  the Google Fonts CSS request. The source header logo is reduced from roughly
  192 KiB to 51 KiB, with responsive image sizes and a social-sharing image.
- First-page attribution in memory across client navigation. The existing
  Formspree submission includes landing_page. A hudson:lead browser event fires
  once after confirmed form success; errors are visible and do not count as leads.
  The event contains page paths and method only, without submitted names,
  emails or message content. It does not send data to an analytics provider.

Search intent map

Homepage: virtual assistant support for small businesses and charities.
Services overview: compare the available kinds of business support.
Executive administration: inbox, diary, correspondence and meeting assistance.
Board support: papers, meeting administration, documentation and minutes.
Personal assistance: schedules, travel, appointments and personal administration.
Operations management: workflows, processes, CRM and project coordination.
Event management: event planning administration, supplier liaison and logistics.
Bespoke support: a mixed scope or specific administrative project.
Pricing: compare on-demand, monthly and project arrangements.
Contact: enquire or discuss a free consultation.

These are descriptive content targets based on current services, not claims
that keyword volumes or ranking difficulty have been researched.

Owner actions after release

1. Verify the production domain in Google Search Console and submit sitemap.xml.
   Inspect the homepage, services overview and one service detail page.
2. Connect the hudson:lead event to the chosen analytics system under the site's
   consent policy. Treat it as a successful enquiry, not a qualified lead.
   Qualification and won-client outcomes need the owner's CRM or enquiry process.
3. In Formspree, compare enquiries by landing_page. This attribution covers
   navigation within one loaded site session and resets on reload; it does not
   identify an organic source or provide multi-session marketing attribution.
4. Replace or expand existing feedback with client-approved case studies that
   explain the task, work performed and evidenced outcome. Confirm permission
   and accuracy before publishing any client identity, quote or numerical result.
5. Use Search Console impressions and enquiries to refine service titles and
   content. Assess suitable enquiries and clients acquired alongside traffic.
6. Measure production Core Web Vitals after sufficient traffic. Source and build
   improvements do not establish field LCP, INP or CLS results or ranking gains.

The earlier private-dashboard authentication recommendations belong to a
different repository and are outside this public-site change.

Verification

TypeScript and the production build pass. Browser checks cover all 13 public
pages, unique titles, canonical and Open Graph URLs, structured data, sitemap
and robots responses, the apex redirect and an unknown-service 404. Keyboard,
touch and mobile checks pass. Service and FAQ content is available without
JavaScript. Contact success and failure were verified with mocked Formspree
responses; no real enquiry was sent. Browser checks reported no page errors.
Actual email delivery, Search Console indexing and production field performance
require their live accounts or deployed-site data and have not been claimed.
